Deal or No Deal: Playable Studio Experience

Built an editable Unreal Engine 5.7 interpretation of the classic Deal or No Deal studio and a complete 26-case, nine-round single-player game. The browser edition renders the Blender-authored set in Three.js and includes Banker offers, four cameras, keyboard and touch controls, and English and Chinese interfaces.
