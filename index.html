RISA Preprint · 2026 · Co-first author

Disagree to Explore, Agree to Commit

Routing-Guided Test-Time Scaling for Software Agents

Uses a model’s expert-routing signals to guide a software agent’s next action and select a final code patch, without an external judge or model-weight updates.

44.9% → 48.2% macro-average resolved rate on SWE-bench Verified across six gpt-oss conditions, comparing uniform selection with routing arbitration.

TraceGraph Preprint · 2026 · Co-first author

Shared Decision Landscapes for Agents

Diagnosing and improving agent trajectories

Maps agents’ actions and observations into a shared graph to identify productive paths, recurring traps, and opportunities for recovery.

Five benchmark splits. Profiles access, trap exposure, and repair to reveal differences that aggregate success rates conceal.

SliceGraph Preprint · 2026 · Co-first author

Same Answer, Different Reasoning Paths

An activation-based atlas of reasoning processes

Groups chain-of-thought traces by their intermediate activation patterns to study how correct solutions diverge and reconnect along the way.

85.5% of 954 problem–model cells contain correct, same-answer runs that belong to multiple process families.
